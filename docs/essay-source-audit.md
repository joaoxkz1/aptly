No replacement questions were added. The original per-question report, [essay-diagram-audit.md](essay-diagram-audit.md), records the earlier audit; this report records the subsequent source and outcome-depth corrections.

These are **Aptly-authored questions and assessment judgments**, not IB questions or IB-approved markschemes. Sources establish syllabus and assessment boundaries. The per-ID economic reasoning below is our evaluation of our own prompts, not a reproduction or paraphrase of an IB question bank. Source review does not measure model agreement with teachers or establish handwriting accuracy.

## Review decisions

- **Required depth:** AO2 means explanation/application/analysis; AO3 includes synthesis/evaluation. A 15-mark evaluation of a standalone AO2 outcome is retired unless the actual prompt and guidance genuinely assess a supported AO3 outcome. Adding an optional policy comment to guidance does not by itself fix an over-demanding prompt.
- **Cross-topic reasoning:** a bank topic is an indexing label, not the complete syllabus boundary. The evidence column identifies specific cross-topic outcomes where they justify an integrated task. A generic appeal to “economic evaluation” is insufficient.
- **Diagram roles:** N is `necessary_for_task`, an Aptly judgment that a diagram normally carries a central demanded mechanism; O is `optional_appropriate`. A family is an appropriate starting point, not a whitelist. Valid economic alternatives remain creditable. The holistic grading contract controls the result, with no automatic submark split.
- **Retirement:** retired IDs and original prompts remain readable for historical provenance. They are excluded from new bank selection and current reuse; stored grading snapshots are not rewritten. A row's retained historical diagram role does not reactivate it.
- **Empirical limits:** this audit closes the source-resolvable curriculum and authoring questions recorded here. Independent teacher-marked response calibration and authentic handwriting evaluation are separate empirical work; they are not substitutes for the source corrections.

## Reconciled result

All **211** original essays remain identifiable: **186 active**, **25 retired** (23 outcome-depth mismatches and two price-discrimination essays). The associated short-question check retires two additional definitions, producing **27 retired / 371 active** across the full 398-record bank.

Across all historical essay IDs, diagram judgments are **80 N / 131 O**. Across active essays, they are **69 N / 117 O**. Active essays comprise **144 shared SL/HL and 42 HL-only**. Four explicit level corrections are included: firms' influence on demand, firms' use of YED, automatic stabilizers, and exchange-rate-regime evaluation. Existing topic 2.4 HL classifications remain unchanged.

Source concerns were reconciled against the actual prompts by the coordinating agent and independent research/question-bank reviewers. One proposed retirement was reversed: `econ-v1-1.1-15-001` genuinely integrates growth's distributional and environmental consequences. No numerical count target determined which questions remained active.
