# Manual essay diagram resolution fix

16 September 2026. Narrow follow-up to the production pollution/market-failure failure. No paid model or image calls were made.

## Cause and authority paths

Audited bank essays use their saved private `diagramRequirement`, with the per-ID audit as a historical fallback. Recognized saved Practice loads that guidance through the owner's session. Both manual Paper 1(a) and Paper 1(b) previously used a diagram-name regular expression and a broad command-word check. Production/pollution wording missed the model-name expression, so it silently became `optional_appropriate`. Holistic reconciliation also did not guarantee an omission diagnostic, leaving Next Step without a supported priority.

## Correction

- Server-owned manual resolution now uses an exact normalized match to an active audited question (including total, framework and context), explicit task instructions, sourced task exceptions, and bounded multi-clause economic mechanisms. It never reads the answer or browser-selected role. There is no additional AI classifier.
- Pollution causing production externalities/market failure resolves to `necessary_for_task`, with MSC/MPC, market/social output and welfare-loss expectations. Negative consumption and positive externalities, market interventions, AD/AS, currency determination, firm output/efficiency, PPC and trade mechanisms have separate recognition boundaries.
- Debt sustainability is `optional`; inflation redistribution is `appropriate_support`; the sourced integration comparison and asymmetric-information responses do not assess an economic diagram. Model limitations, measurement questions, currency price conversion, ambiguous/contradictory instructions and unrecognized demands do not inherit a requirement from topic words.
- Uncertainty becomes `unresolved` with a provisional estimate, not a verified diagram exemption. Provisional UI copy distinguishes uncertain task guidance from an inferred mark total. This remains a bounded recognizer, not a claim to authenticate every arbitrary question or unpublished markscheme.
- The frozen holistic prompt treats a missing necessary diagram as a meaningful unmet element when choosing the best-fit band and position. It imposes no fixed deduction, score target or universal band ceiling. Reconciliation preserves the grader's mark and adds only truthful omission diagnostics, scoped feedback and diagram-focused Next Step/Current Focus evidence.
- Completed manual requests replay their frozen contract and original result before new omission checks. Owner, request fingerprint and attachment hash must match. Changed requests conflict. Revision comparisons distinguish grading versions; historical rows are never regraded.

The [source notes](manual-essay-diagram-source-notes.md) give exact sources, locators, access limits and confidence. IB mark rules are distinguished from Aptly recognition/enum judgments. The supplied prose is a verbatim fixture; surrounding suggested marks and commentary do not enter grader inputs or expected-score assertions.
