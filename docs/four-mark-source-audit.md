## What is a source rule, and what is authored judgment?

**Source rule (E26):** Paper 2(c–f) commonly separates visual and explanatory evidence. It allows valid context-consistent alternatives, carries one error forward within a part, limits independently valid but incompatible mechanisms, and accepts movement established in prose. Its labeling guidance permits conventional generic notation and requires benefit of the doubt. Its Paper 2(g) guidance does not impose a diagram prerequisite for the top band.

**Aptly adoption:** All 80 original diagram tasks explicitly adopt the 2+2 contract and overall 3/4 essential-label ceiling. That includes custom HL and introductory practice: this is transparent reuse of the marking structure, not a claim those HL topics occur in Paper 2. E26 expressly states the ceiling; M23 repeats it, whereas the inspected M25 examples omit that older boilerplate. The audit does not claim every published markscheme prints it.

**Corroboration:** M23 p.5 and M25 p.10 both allow an unlabeled Lorenz equality diagonal; this prompted the two Lorenz corrections. M25 p.9 corroborates closed causal poverty loops and agreement between visual and written mechanisms. No axes are required for circular-flow or poverty-cycle diagrams. Conceptual nodes, directed flows and task-relevant changes are their meaningful labels.

**Authored judgment:** Every exact partial-credit boundary and hypothetical scenario below is Aptly's. The guide supports scope and level, not these exact words or thresholds. Economic conclusions in the per-ID records are independent checks of the supplied assumptions. Alternative geometry, abbreviated labels, axis orientation and equivalent coherent reasoning receive credit where their meaning is clear. No title, decorative arrow, welfare shading, calculation, evaluation or recommendation is imposed unless the particular task asks for that evidence.
