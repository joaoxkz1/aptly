## Evidence used

### E26 — current Paper 1 approach

[2026 Economics examiner instructions](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/index.html?chapter=3&doc=EX_instructions_2026_e&part=10), sections **Assessment criteria**, **Notes on marking HL/SL paper 1**, **Part (a)** and **Part (b)**; the [indexed generated PDF](https://ibpublishing.ibo.org/exinst/apps/exinst/pdf.html?chapter=3&doc=EX_instructions_2026_e&part=10) locates the Paper 1 discussion on p.4.

Part (a) expects students, **“when necessary, to produce economic diagrams to support their explanations.”** Examiners combine question-specific guidance with markbands and use best fit because a response need not meet every descriptor. The assessment guidance prohibits reductions without authority in the criteria or supplementary guidance. Diagrams must match the task and the student's analysis. Part (b) connects diagrams to relevant analysis, evaluation and real-world examples, while accepting valid alternative approaches.

**Conclusion:** omission of a task-relevant necessary diagram must enter holistic judgment, but neither a fixed deduction nor a universal numerical maximum follows. A Paper 1 label alone does not establish necessity. This is source-verified framework guidance; the four-role enum is Aptly's representation.

### N20-SL — the reported pollution task

[November 2020 SL Paper 1 markscheme](https://papers.xtremepape.rs/IB/2020%20Examination%20Session/November%202020%20Examination%20Session/Individuals%20and%20societies/Economics_paper_1__SL_markscheme.pdf), **N20/3/ECONO/SP1/ENG/TZ0/XX/M**, Q1(a), p.3. [Independent mirror of the same document](https://dl.ibdocs.re/IB%20PAST%20PAPERS%20-%20YEAR/2020%20Examination%20Session/November%202020%20Examination%20Session/Individuals%20and%20societies/Economics_paper_1__SL_markscheme.pdf).

This is the exact question supplied in the defect report. Expected content includes **“diagram to show an over-allocation of resources where MSC is greater than MPC”**, alongside the explanation of production pollution and excessive output. The historical scheme also specifically limits a *negative-consumption-externality diagram* to its level 2. That is a different error from an omitted diagram and uses historical bands; it must not be copied into a universal current omission ceiling.

**Conclusion:** the negative-production-externality mechanism materially calls for the MSC/MPC model and excess market output relative to the social optimum. The strongest task-specific role is justified by the exact historical scheme plus the current framework. Absence is an unmet relevant element, even if prose is excellent. No numeric mark is determined here. A policy recommendation is outside this explain-only task.

### N22-HL — explicit optional counterexamples

[November 2022 HL Paper 1 markscheme](https://papers.xtremepape.rs/IB/2022%20Examination%20Session/November%202022%20Examination%20Session/Individuals%20and%20societies/Economics_paper_1__HL_markscheme.pdf), **N22/3/ECONO/HP1/ENG/TZ0/XX/M**.

- **Q2(a), p.6:** explaining why sustainable government/national debt is a macroeconomic objective. AD/AS may illustrate government spending, but **“the question can be answered without the use of a diagram.”** The explanation concerns servicing, creditworthiness and future fiscal choices. This is an explicit optional-diagram case and an HL question, not evidence that all debt-related macroeconomic mechanisms are diagram-free.
- **Q3(a), p.9:** comparing a free trade area and a common market can also be answered without a diagram; a Venn representation may help communicate their differences. This does not establish that tariff removal, trade creation or trade diversion is diagram-free.

**Conclusion:** excellent prose remains eligible for the top band in these tasks. Topic words alone must not force a diagram. The optional role is directly supported; exact paraphrase matching remains Aptly engineering judgment.

### N25-HL — useful support without necessity, and current bands

[November 2025 HL Paper 1 TZ1 markscheme](https://papers.xtremepape.rs/IB/2025%20Examination%20Session/November%202025%20Examination%20Session/Individuals%20and%20societies/Economics_paper_1_TZ1_HL_markscheme.pdf), **8825–5202M**, Q2(a), p.6.

The task explains inflation's redistributive effects. The scheme anticipates AD/AS illustration but explicitly says **“a diagram is not necessary for this question.”** The relevant reasoning distinguishes borrowers/lenders, bargaining power and fixed incomes. This supports a useful-but-nonessential regression: `appropriate_support` may record the diagram's illustrative usefulness, but omission alone must not bar a top-band response. The distinction between this enum and `optional` is Aptly's descriptive choice, not separate official mark rules.

The same page's current Paper 1(a) bands qualify diagram use by appropriateness, progressing from inclusion to explanation to full explanation. Q1(a), p.3, expects the monopoly abnormal-profit model; Q2(b), p.7, expects AD/AS when evaluating fiscal-policy disinflation. These comparisons show why a redistributive-inflation exception must not erase material diagram expectations for other inflation or market-power tasks.

### SP22 — a diagram is not needed

[First-assessment-2022 specimens and markschemes](https://ibdocs.re/p/IB%20SPECIMEN%20PAPERS/Group%203%20-%20Individuals%20and%20Societies/Economics/Specimen%20Papers%202022%20-%20English.pdf), **SPEC/3/ECONO/HP1/ENG/TZ0/XX/M**, Q1(a), markscheme p.5 (**combined PDF zero-based page 8**).

For explaining two government responses to asymmetric information, the scheme states **“Diagram: not needed for this question.”** It instead points to adverse selection/moral hazard and responses through information, regulation or legislation. This supports a no-diagram task family despite being an HL market-failure intervention question. A broad rule equating market failure, government intervention or HL with necessary diagrams would be wrong.

## Boundaries of the conclusion

- **Source-verified:** current best-fit procedure; exact historical pollution model; explicit no-diagram debt, integration, redistribution and asymmetric-information examples.
- **Strongly corroborated:** applying the pollution model expectation under the current Paper 1 framework, where the economic mechanism is unchanged.
- **Aptly engineering judgment:** enum names, deterministic recognition boundaries, confidence/provisional handling, prompt constraints and feedback routing. None is an IB-issued policy.
- **Empirical calibration:** the exact holistic mark for any particular excellent-prose/absent-diagram response and model consistency. No paid assessment or image call was made for this review, and no exact mark is forecast.

The source review establishes that uncertainty must not be expressed as a verified absence of diagram requirements. It does not authenticate an unpublished question-specific markscheme for every possible manual prompt.
