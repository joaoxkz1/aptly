## Scope and assessment basis

Reviewed all **211 existing 10/15-mark bank questions**, including each public prompt and its private theory, analysis, application, evaluation, alternative-route and misconception guidance. Review date: 2026-09-15. This is an Aptly authoring audit, **not independent teacher calibration or an official IB per-question markscheme**. No original question or historical blueprint is rewritten by the audit data.

At this initial stage, the requirements were recorded in the data-only server export [essay-diagram-audit.ts](../lib/assessment/question-bank/economics-v1/essay-diagram-audit.ts). The root-owned [trusted-contract.ts](../lib/assessment/trusted-contract.ts) resolved them before seeing a student's answer or attachment, and new saved essay blueprints then used `economics-essay-blueprint-v2`. Current version routing and revised authoring data are documented in [essay-source-audit.md](essay-source-audit.md) and [diagram-aware-grading.md](diagram-aware-grading.md); stored historical requirements retain precedence over later audit changes.

The guide's applicable 10/15-mark descriptors assess relevant diagrams within a best-fit judgment. The audit identifies when the exact task needs a graphical relationship and when a diagram is an appropriate supporting choice. **Neither role adds a fixed diagram allocation, numerical missing-diagram cap, or universal top-band gate.** A valid alternative model can satisfy the exact demand. A suggested family is a primary model to consider, not an exclusive allowed answer. A null family means no single model is prescribed.

Current source closure is documented in [diagram-assessment-basis.md](diagram-assessment-basis.md) and [essay-source-audit.md](essay-source-audit.md), including the amended guide and indexed official 2026 examiner instructions. The initial access limitation has been resolved through those sources. No Paper 2(g) question occurs among these 211 Paper 1 entries; its separate rules do not determine their diagram roles.

## Material corrections and review boundaries

- Demand/supply adjustment was missed where the prompt said shortage, surplus, production costs or technology rather than the resolver's narrow named-model phrases. For example, `econ-v1-2.3-10-001`, `002` and `003` now carry task-specific demand/supply requirements.
- `econ-v1-2.11-10-002` needs cost/revenue analysis of market power even though the prompt does not spell out monopoly.
- `econ-v1-3.2-10-001` and `002` require AD/AS; `econ-v1-3.6-10-001` uses the phrase deflationary gap, missed by the initial recessionary-gap expression.
- `econ-v1-4.1-10-001` requires the comparative-advantage PPC relationship; `econ-v1-4.4-10-001` requires trade-creation analysis.
- `econ-v1-4.2-10-003` uses international trade analysis for an export subsidy, not an ordinary domestic subsidy family. For `econ-v1-4.5-10-002`, domestic inflation following depreciation is primarily AD/AS; a foreign-exchange model can support a coherent alternative path.
- `econ-v1-4.3-10-001` asks about the infant-industry argument. Its protective mechanism may be illustrated with a trade diagram, but that diagram is not inherently needed to explain the argument's assumptions and logic. It is optional.
- Broad institutional, measurement, behavioral and development judgments remain optional when no particular graphical relationship is essential to the task. Each 15-mark prompt was reviewed separately; a shared topic does not impose one role on all its essays.
- The initial price-discrimination and XED scope flags are now resolved in [essay-source-audit.md](essay-source-audit.md): dedicated unsupported records are retired from current selection, and broad active guidance no longer requires those concepts. Historical prompts and completed snapshots remain identifiable.

## Contract and persistence limits

The tests in [trusted-contract.test.ts](../lib/assessment/trusted-contract.test.ts) cover bank coverage, task-specific overrides, authored/inferred provenance, written four-mark and definition behavior, known paper/part and SL/HL metadata, Paper 2(g), missing or placeholder stimulus, and contract/version/context/answer hash separation. They are local engineering checks with synthetic inputs. Existing combined-grading and persistence suites cover saved mark reconciliation and database integration.

Validation on 2026-09-15: all **44 contract tests passed**; the joint contract, component-scoring, assessed-visual-schema and combined-assessment run passed **170/170 tests**. TypeScript checking (`npx tsc --noEmit --pretty false`) also passed. The checks exposed and verified fixes for authored null-family precedence and source-required metadata on Paper 2(g)/3(b). No paid model calls or teacher marking occurred.

A contract is a trusted assessment definition, not an authenticated IB markscheme. Manual tasks retain inferred-practice provenance. The minimum source-text gate rejects empty or bare references; it cannot establish that a substantial pasted source is the correct original stimulus. Source relevance, legibility and unresolved ambiguity must still be handled by the review flow. Historical completed assessments keep their stored result; new assessments use a fresh immutable attempt snapshot.
