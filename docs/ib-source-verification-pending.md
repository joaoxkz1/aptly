## Evidence classes

- **Source-verified:** applicable IB-authored rule or outcome read using the access recorded in the basis.
- **Strongly corroborated:** analogous IB examples support an interpretation but do not become an official scheme for an original Aptly question.
- **Aptly judgment:** original question wording, partial boundary, inferred contract or software behavior.
- **Empirical calibration:** accuracy and agreement evidence requiring independent marking or authentic response samples.

A source-verified rule does not make an Aptly-authored question an official examination question. Equally, the absence of teacher labels does not reopen a completed source review.
