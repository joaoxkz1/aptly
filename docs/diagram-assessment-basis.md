This record distinguishes four evidence classes:

- **Source-verified:** the applicable IB-authored rule or syllabus outcome was read, with the access method recorded.
- **Strongly corroborated:** comparable IB examples support an interpretation, without being a markscheme for Aptly's original question.
- **Aptly judgment:** an original task, partial-credit boundary, inferred allocation or implementation choice.
- **Empirical calibration:** agreement with independent marking and performance on authentic student work; source reading and software tests cannot establish it.

## Manual contracts, versions and remaining calibration

A recognized manually entered diagram/explanation task receives an **inferred practice estimate**. Server ownership, a total of four marks, and the use of verified general rules do not authenticate a missing question-specific IB markscheme. An unknown future question's unpublished scheme is not a source document that can be retrospectively “verified” now. Its provenance and provisional scoring state remain explicit; source-dependent tasks still require usable context.

The source-closed versions are `ib-econ-2026-v3`, `economics-grading-blueprint-v2`, `economics-four-mark-blueprint-v2`, `economics-essay-blueprint-v3` and `inferred-question-contract-v2`. Migration [0015](../supabase/migrations/0015_source_reviewed_contracts.sql) widens accepted identifiers while preserving historical rows and snapshots. This basis does not assert that a migration or deployment ran; the implementation validation record owns operational evidence.

Immutable snapshots, private blueprints, image/transcription separation, deterministic arithmetic, retry identity, failure handling, analytics exclusion and frozen historical results are Aptly engineering choices. Their tests establish software behavior. The [closed synthetic live run](diagram-live-validation/README.md) and [offline checks](diagram-local-testing.md) do not establish authentic-student accuracy or examiner agreement.

The [calibration procedure](diagram-calibration.md) remains useful post-implementation empirical work: independent blinded marking, authentic handwriting, difficult photographs and borderline response interpretation. That work is classified separately; it is not an unresolved source-access question or a blanket teacher-approval prerequisite for the source-reviewed implementation. No teacher labels, agreement statistics or additional paid runs are claimed here.
